About

Northern Solutions L.L.C. develops advanced computational solutions for scientific research, engineering, and materials innovation.

Scientific Computing

We specialize in computational modeling of materials and molecular systems using atomistic simulation, electronic-structure methods, molecular dynamics, and high-performance scientific computing.

Physics-Based Modeling

Our work connects first-principles calculations, classical atomistic simulations, and data-driven methods to investigate structure, thermodynamics, transport, diffusion, interfaces, and materials properties across multiple length and time scales.

Scientific Software

We develop, optimize, deploy, and support scientific software and computational workflows for modern CPU- and GPU-based high-performance computing environments.

Scientific Capabilities

From quantum-mechanical calculations to large-scale molecular dynamics and machine-learning interatomic potentials, Northern Solutions supports the complete scientific simulation workflow.

Research & Engineering Applications

Our computational methods can be applied across materials science, chemistry, energy storage, molecular systems, and advanced engineering.

Machine-Learning Interatomic Potentials

Development of machine-learning potentials trained on first-principles data for large-scale molecular dynamics simulations with near-DFT accuracy.

Hydrogen Diffusion in Metals

First-principles and atomistic simulations of hydrogen migration, trapping, diffusion barriers, local bonding, and electronic interactions in metallic materials.

Battery Electrolytes

Molecular simulation and force-field development for lithium-ion electrolytes, including ion solvation, transport properties, diffusion, conductivity, and concentrated electrolyte environments.

Metals & Alloys

Atomistic and electronic-structure modeling of metals, high-entropy alloys, defects, interfaces, structural stability, transport, and mechanical behavior.

Interfaces & Molecular Systems

Molecular-level investigation of liquids, polymers, ionic liquids, surfaces, interfaces, and complex molecular environments.

HPC Scientific Environments

Reproducible and optimized computational environments for scientific applications using MPI, GPUs, containers, and modern HPC schedulers.

Philosophy

Scientific software must be accurate, reproducible, scalable, and grounded in the underlying physics.


Northern Solutions combines scientific understanding with modern computational methods to build reliable simulation workflows and research software. Our approach emphasizes numerical accuracy, reproducibility, efficient use of high-performance computing resources, transparent methodology, and maintainable software.


Our goal is to help researchers and engineering organizations move efficiently from scientific questions to computational models, large-scale simulations, data analysis, and actionable scientific insight.
